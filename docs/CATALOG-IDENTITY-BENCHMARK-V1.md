# Podium 7 Catalog Identity Golden Benchmark V1

Status: **multi-slice source-backed regression benchmark; not a production-quality estimate**.

## Purpose

The benchmark measures the conservative Catalog Identity resolver against human-curated expected outcomes that are stored independently of resolver output.

Datasets:

```text
benchmarks/catalog_identity_golden_v1.json
benchmarks/catalog_identity_golden_br_v1.json
benchmarks/catalog_identity_year_semantics_challenge_v1.json
```

Runner:

```text
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/run_catalog_identity_benchmark.py
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/run_catalog_identity_benchmark.py --dataset benchmarks/catalog_identity_golden_br_v1.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/run_catalog_identity_benchmark.py --dataset benchmarks/catalog_identity_year_semantics_challenge_v1.json
```

The runner emits a JSON report and does not mutate the dataset or persistence.

## Seed composition

The original version `1.0` contains 12 automotive identity pairs:

```text
4 MATCH
4 NO_MATCH
4 REVIEW
```

The cases are anchored to seven manufacturer sources from Toyota, Ford, Porsche and BMW. Source URLs and a short statement of what each source supports are stored directly in the dataset.

This remains a deliberately small seed. Twelve curated pairs are useful for regression and policy validation, but they are not a statistically representative estimate of production precision or recall.

## Brazil slice

Version `br-1.0` adds a separate 12-case Brazilian-market slice, preserving the original seed unchanged:

```text
4 MATCH
4 NO_MATCH
4 REVIEW
```

Coverage includes Toyota Corolla Cross, Chevrolet Onix, Volkswagen T-Cross, Fiat Strada and FIPE/Chevrolet Corsa cases. The slice exercises:

- Brazilian trim and powertrain distinctions;
- incomplete-source duplicates where trim evidence is missing;
- transmission as an identity-defining difference;
- model-year contradiction despite a shared FIPE code;
- shared FIPE code without enough structural evidence remaining `REVIEW`.

The primary vehicle/configuration sources are Toyota, Chevrolet, Volkswagen and Fiat/Stellantis first-party material. FIPE semantics are grounded in the official FIPE vehicle-index page. One explicitly labeled secondary source is used only to enumerate the historical Corsa Wind FIPE code across model years; the benchmark does not treat that secondary source as a stronger identity authority than FIPE itself.

Together, the two original golden slices provide 24 curated pairs, balanced overall at 8 `MATCH`, 8 `NO_MATCH` and 8 `REVIEW`. The slices are evaluated independently so a regional expansion cannot silently rewrite the baseline of the original seed.

## Year-semantics regression slice

Version `year-semantics-1.1` is a separate six-case source-backed regression slice grounded in Senatran/SERPRO, FIPE and Toyota do Brasil primary sources. It protects the product decision to keep manufacture year and model year separate while avoiding unsupported automatic matches when model-year evidence is incomplete.

```text
2 MATCH
3 NO_MATCH
1 REVIEW
```

The selected policy requires all six labels exactly. Explicit non-overlap in manufacture year or model year is a deterministic contradiction. Missing manufacture-year evidence alone may still match when model-year and required structural evidence agree. If model year is explicit on only one side, the pair routes to `REVIEW` unless stronger identity evidence establishes the match.

The detailed evidence and rule are recorded in [`CATALOG-YEAR-SEMANTICS-CHALLENGE-V1.md`](CATALOG-YEAR-SEMANTICS-CHALLENGE-V1.md). The filename retains `challenge` for continuity with the characterization work that produced the decision; the contents are now a regression gate.

## Curation rule

Expected outcomes are product decisions, not labels generated by `resolve_catalog_pair()`.

- `MATCH`: the two records represent the same catalog identity with enough deterministic evidence for automatic merge.
- `NO_MATCH`: the records contain an explicit identity contradiction such as generation, variant, transmission or an explicit non-overlapping year dimension.
- `REVIEW`: available evidence is insufficient or too generic to justify either automatic merge or deterministic rejection.

The benchmark includes same-identity aliases, generation contradictions, trim/powertrain distinctions, deliberately incomplete records, real FIPE-as-supporting cases and the selected manufacture/model-year policy. Synthetic namespace-strength edge cases remain covered by focused V2.1 decision tests.

## Metrics

The evaluator reports:

- `falseMergeCount` / `falseMergeRate`: expected `NO_MATCH` predicted as `MATCH`;
- `missedDuplicateCount` / `missedDuplicateRate`: expected `MATCH` predicted as `NO_MATCH`;
- `matchPrecision`: expected `MATCH` among all predicted `MATCH`;
- `matchRecall`: predicted `MATCH` among all expected `MATCH`;
- `reviewRate`: fraction of all cases predicted `REVIEW`;
- `ambiguousOvercommitCount` / rate: expected `REVIEW` predicted as either `MATCH` or `NO_MATCH`;
- overall exact-label accuracy for regression visibility.

A resolver that sends a positive pair to `REVIEW` hurts match recall but is not counted as a missed duplicate. A resolver that sends an ambiguous pair to `MATCH` or `NO_MATCH` is counted as overcommit. These distinctions preserve the product's conservative posture.

## Priority

Error priority remains:

```text
false merges > missed duplicates > review volume
```

Reducing `REVIEW` is not an objective if it increases false merges or unsupported deterministic decisions.

## CI role

Each original golden slice has two focused regression tests:

1. dataset structure/composition remains source-backed and balanced;
2. the current golden baseline does not regress into false merge, missed duplicate or ambiguous overcommit on those curated cases.

The year-semantics slice has its own source-backed structure check and exact six-case regression test. A mismatch is now a product regression, not merely a characterization result.

These tests protect known product decisions. They are not production release thresholds and must not be presented as statistical evidence that Podium 7 has perfect identity resolution.

## Expansion plan

Grow the dataset by evidence, not by volume alone. Useful next additions include:

- adjacent model-year transitions where equipment or powertrain changes mid-generation;
- Brazilian market-specific naming differences across manufacturer, FIPE and dealer/source labels;
- more FIPE examples across multiple manufacturers and body styles;
- real external identifiers classified as `STRONG` and `REFERENCE_ONLY`;
- hard negatives that share make/model/generation/powertrain but differ in identity-defining trim;
- cross-source duplicates where one source is materially incomplete in year or transmission evidence.

As coverage grows, benchmark reports can support meaningful trend comparison and later release thresholds. Thresholds should be chosen only after the dataset is large and representative enough to justify them.
